# International / Geographic Replication

## Trigger
Material expansion outside the proven home geography or into economically distinct regions.

## Replication evidence
By country/cluster/cohort, test:
- product-market fit and repeat behavior;
- local customer job/occasion;
- channel mix and availability;
- competitive set;
- regulatory/administrative barriers;
- supply chain/geographic constraints;
- pricing/income differences;
- early productivity/unit evidence.

## CAGE + AAA routing
Use CAGE to identify relevant cultural, administrative, geographic, and economic distance. Then describe whether the company responds through Adaptation, Aggregation, and/or Arbitrage. These frameworks explain strategy; they do not prove success.

## Status
`PROVEN`, `DEVELOPING`, `UNPROVEN`, `FAILED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

## Failure modes
- Foreign store/capacity count doubles, therefore replication is proven.
- Home-market economics copied into a foreign market without localization/cost evidence.

## Handoff
Layer 1 evaluates replication of demand/competitive position. Layer 2 evaluates replicated unit economics; Layer 3 evaluates financeable runway.
