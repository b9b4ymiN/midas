# Competitive Position & Share-Gain Mechanism

## Trigger
Always.

## Competitive system
Separate:
- direct competitors;
- substitutes from Layer 0 customer jobs/occasions;
- emerging entrants/business models;
- structural analogues used only for lessons/reference classes.

## Required analysis
1. Define the market-share denominator and time period.
2. Compare company growth with category and key competitors.
3. Identify the mechanism that should change share: product value, price/cost, availability/distribution, brand, switching, network/data, supply chain, regulation, capacity, execution, or other evidenced mechanism.
4. Seek observable outcomes: share trend, penetration, productivity, retention, pricing, distribution, cohort evidence, relative economics.
5. Test whether share was purchased through discounting, excess capacity, M&A, subsidies, or deteriorating economics.

## Mechanism evidence
`PROVEN`, `DEVELOPING`, `WEAK`, `UNRESOLVED`.

## Important rule
**Low current market share is not evidence of future share gain.** Share is an outcome; the thesis requires a causal mechanism plus evidence.

## Handoff
Do not declare moat/ROIIC from share alone. Pass relative economic questions downstream.
