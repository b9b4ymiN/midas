# Growth Execution & Evidence Trajectory

## Trigger
Material growth initiative or management claim about category, channels, products, capacity, or geography.

## Promise → Action → Result
For each thesis-critical initiative record:
1. **Promise** — dated management statement/target.
2. **Action** — capital, hiring, launches, partnerships, capacity, stores, contracts, product releases, regulatory steps.
3. **Result** — adoption, revenue/volume, share/productivity, retention, customer evidence, or failure/delay.

Management intention is not the same as operating evidence.

## Evidence trajectory
`RAPIDLY_IMPROVING`, `IMPROVING`, `STABLE`, `DETERIORATING`, `UNRESOLVED`.

## Failure modes
- Repeating a three-year target as a fact.
- Treating a delayed rollout as proof of market rejection without examining the cause.
- Grading management charisma instead of observable execution.

## Handoff
Pass execution evidence and misses forward. Capital-allocation quality remains owned by Reinvestment & Runway.
