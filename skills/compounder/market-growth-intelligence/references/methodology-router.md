# Layer 1 Methodology Router

Frameworks are question-specific tools. **Do not run them all mechanically.**

| Investment question | Trigger | Method / source | Required evidence | Output | Main failure mode | Stop boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Are historical KPIs comparable? | Any multi-period trend | Metric Comparability Gate | definitions, reporting notes, mix, consolidation, accounting | comparability status | false trend from changed definitions | do not normalize without evidence |
| Is the demand pool structurally growing? | Always | demand-driver decomposition + Jobs-to-Be-Done when needed | category volumes, penetration, usage, pricing, substitution, macro/regulatory drivers | category regime + demand drivers | consultant TAM treated as demand evidence | no valuation/TAM extrapolation beyond Layer 0 scope |
| Who captures the market's profit? | Value chain non-obvious / platform / franchise / regulated sectors | Porter + Profit Pools | value-chain economics, bargaining power, margins, fees, concentration | profit-pool map + structural direction | revenue growth assumed to equal profit growth | no full company ROIC analysis |
| Is economic value moving to a new business design? | Structural disruption or new route-to-market | Slywotzky Value Migration | customer shift, share, profit, business-model changes | inflow/stable/outflow hypothesis | thematic storytelling without operating evidence | keep hypothesis explicit |
| Why should share increase/decrease? | Always | Mauboussin market-share lens + company evidence | share trend, relative growth, availability, pricing, retention, economics | share mechanism + evidence class | low share = runway; discount-funded share | economics handed to Layer 2 |
| Where did reported growth come from? | Always | McKinsey GGD + operating bridge | segment/category/geography growth, M&A, company metrics | growth attribution | double counting market/share/channel/store effects | preserve unresolved residual |
| Does a new product/channel create growth or move it? | Material channel/product change | net incrementality + cross-channel effects | buyers, occasions, recapture, channel mix, margins | incrementality status | channel GMV counted as additive | full margin/ROI belongs downstream |
| Is a consumer brand broadening demand? | Consumer/retail brand | CEP + Mental/Physical Availability | penetration, buyer growth, availability, occasions | demand/availability evidence | loyalty story without penetration | N/A for non-consumer businesses |
| Can home-market success replicate abroad? | Material international expansion | CAGE + AAA | country cohorts, localization, channels, competition, regulation, early productivity | replication status | foreign store/capacity count = proven success | full unit returns downstream |
| Is management executing the growth strategy? | Material stated growth initiative | Promise → Action → Result | dated promises, capex/launches, operational outcomes | execution + evidence trajectory | charisma/narrative as execution | no broad management-quality score |

## Always-on core
1. Metric Comparability Gate
2. Demand & Category Evolution
3. Competitive Position / Share Mechanism
4. Growth Decomposition

## Conditional modules
- Industry Profit Pool / Five Forces: when value capture or bargaining power is material.
- Consumer CEP/availability: consumer brand only.
- Channel incrementality: material channel/product route changes.
- CAGE/AAA: material cross-border growth.
- Value migration: disruptive business-design shift.

## Required evidence discipline
- Prefer company filings, regulator/government statistics, recognized industry datasets, and primary competitor filings.
- Consultant market-size estimates are `MARKET_EXPECTATION/ESTIMATE`, not FACT about future demand.
- A market-share claim without denominator definition and period is `UNRESOLVED` or qualified.
- A channel-growth claim without cannibalization evidence cannot be labeled highly incremental.
- An international-growth claim without local cohort/adoption evidence cannot be labeled PROVEN replication.
