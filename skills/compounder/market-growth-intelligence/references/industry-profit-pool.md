# Industry Structure, Profit Pool & Value Migration

## Trigger
Use when revenue growth may accrue to different actors, bargaining power is material, or a new business design is changing value capture.

## Method
1. Draw the value chain and material actors.
2. Identify where revenue and profit are generated today.
3. Evaluate rivalry, entrants, substitutes, supplier power, and buyer/channel power.
4. Ask how those forces are changing, not only their current state.
5. Track whether profit is moving between layers/business designs.

## Profit-pool direction
`INFLOW`, `STABLE`, `OUTFLOW`, `MIXED`, `UNRESOLVED`.

## Evidence
Margins/fees, take rates, supplier economics, franchise economics, channel commissions, concentration, switching behavior, regulation, capacity, and relative return evidence where public.

## Failure modes
- Category +20% therefore company profit +20%.
- Large revenue pool assumed to be the attractive profit pool.
- “Value migration” declared from a thematic story without share/profit/customer evidence.

## Handoff
Layer 1 explains where profit capture is moving. Layer 2 determines the company-specific return consequences.
