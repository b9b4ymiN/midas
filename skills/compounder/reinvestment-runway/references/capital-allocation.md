# Capital Allocation and Reinvestment Quality

The core business can be excellent while the allocator destroys owner value. Evaluate the engine and the allocator separately.

## Capital allocation map

Reconstruct material uses of internally generated and externally raised capital:
- organic capacity / locations / product investment
- R&D / software / customer acquisition / brand
- working capital
- acquisitions and follow-on integration capital
- debt repayment / new leverage
- repurchases
- dividends
- cash accumulation
- equity issuance / SBC / convertibles

Where possible, reconcile the uses to cash flow statements, balance-sheet changes, acquisition notes, and share-count movements.

## Management behavior test

Evaluate behavior rather than biography:
- stated allocation priorities vs actual deployment
- willingness to stop low-return projects
- hurdle-rate discipline
- leverage behavior across cycles
- incentive structure and time horizon
- related-party transactions / minority treatment
- communication honesty when outcomes miss prior claims

Founder/owner status is a **prior**, not proof.

## Acquisition cohort economics

For material M&A or serial acquirers, evaluate each meaningful acquisition/cohort using the best available lag-adjusted evidence:

`Post-deal incremental NOPAT or FCF attributable to acquired assets / (purchase consideration + assumed debt + follow-on integration/growth capital)`

Also inspect:
- purchase multiple / price paid
- organic growth after acquisition
- margin trajectory
- retention/churn where relevant
- impairment/restructuring charges
- incremental leverage
- dilution

Do not treat EPS accretion, purchase-accounting effects, or revenue growth as proof of acceptable acquisition returns.

## Buybacks and dilution

A repurchase is economically helpful only if:
1. the company has no clearly superior capital use at the margin,
2. repurchase economics are sensible relative to intrinsic/business value expectations, and
3. diluted ownership actually improves after SBC, acquisition issuance, options/RSUs, and other equity issuance.

Record **net diluted-share change**, not buyback headlines alone. If pricing discipline cannot be assessed without a valuation skill, state that limit rather than making a valuation conclusion.

## Output interpretation

Classify allocation behavior as **VALUE_CREATING / MIXED / VALUE_DESTRUCTIVE / UNRESOLVED**, with evidence and major exceptions. This classification is not a numeric score and does not replace the separate core-engine analysis.
