# Emerging Compounder Protocol

Use when consolidated history is short (often ~3–5 years), the company is recently listed, or current accounting returns are depressed by deliberate scaling investment.

## Principle

**Short history lowers Evidence Maturity; it does not automatically lower Compounding Potential.**

## Replace missing long histories with leading evidence

Prioritize where available:
- unit/cohort economics
- mature vs new unit performance
- payback trend
- retention / repeat behavior
- marginal contribution economics
- capacity utilization / ramp curves
- customer/store acquisition efficiency
- evidence trajectory across sequential cohorts/years
- observed economics across geographies, segments, or scale steps

## Young-company future-economics bridge

When current profit/ROIC is immature, use a Damodaran-style economic bridge rather than requiring long reported history:

1. **Revenue opportunity** — serviceable opportunity with realistic penetration, not presentation TAM.
2. **Mature operating economics** — target gross/operating/NOPAT margin anchored to observed cohorts, unit maturity, peers, or explicit assumptions.
3. **Capital efficiency** — sales-to-capital, capital per economic unit, CAC/payback, or another unit-specific capital requirement.
4. **Reinvestment need** — e.g. `ΔRevenue / sales-to-capital` when economically sensible.
5. **Implied future return** — normalized after-tax operating output relative to capital required.

Every target/mature input must be tagged FACT, DERIVED, MANAGEMENT_CLAIM, ESTIMATE, ASSUMPTION, INFERENCE, or UNVERIFIED. Never hide the bridge inside a single projected ROIC.

## Evidence maturity levels

- **Early:** attractive signals but limited cohorts/cycles or disclosure
- **Developing:** multiple cohorts/periods and partial stress evidence
- **Established:** economics demonstrated across cycles/geographies/scale steps
- **Deep:** long, diverse record with strong primary evidence and known failure behavior

## Confidence is separate

Confidence also depends on source quality, measurement quality, model sensitivity, and unresolved unknowns. A company may be `Potential: Exceptional / Evidence Maturity: Early / Confidence: Medium`.

## Anti-patterns

Do not:
- require 10-year ROIC history as an admission ticket
- extrapolate 3 years of revenue CAGR mechanically
- treat management-provided unit economics as audited facts
- assume target mature margins without a bridge from observed cohorts
