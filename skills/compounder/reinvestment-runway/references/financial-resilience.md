# Financial Resilience and Financeable Runway

Market runway is not the same as **financeable reinvestment runway**. A business can have attractive projects but still fail to compound if liquidity, leverage, working capital, or refinancing requirements force it to stop or dilute owners.

## Required checks when material

- operating cash conversion and working-capital intensity
- maintenance needs before growth capital
- cash and committed liquidity
- debt maturity ladder / refinancing concentration
- interest burden and fixed-charge coverage
- covenant or collateral constraints where disclosed
- currency/floating-rate exposure where economically relevant
- external-equity dependence
- dilution required under a reasonable downside/stress path
- cyclicality of cash generation relative to funding needs

## Financeability question

Ask:

> Can the company fund the plausible reinvestment path through a stress period without value-destructive dilution, forced deleveraging, or abandoning high-return projects?

Classify:
- **SELF_FUNDED** — internal cash can fund the material path with adequate resilience.
- **SUPPORTED** — some external funding is required but appears available on tolerable terms.
- **FRAGILE** — runway materially depends on favorable capital markets/refinancing.
- **UNRESOLVED** — evidence is insufficient.

## Interaction with duration

Financial fragility shortens effective duration even when competitive advantage and demand runway are strong. Do not merge this with market TAM; surface it as a distinct constraint.
