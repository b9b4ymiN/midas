# Runway and Duration Methods

## Runway is not TAM

Decompose:

`Headline TAM → Serviceable market → current penetration → realistic share → economic capacity`

Then test whether incremental economics survive expansion.

## Opportunity-set map

Evaluate individually:
1. same product × same geography
2. same product × new geography
3. new product × current customers
4. new customers × existing infrastructure
5. adjacent markets
6. acquisitions
7. network/density effects

For each lane record:
- addressable units/revenue
- capital required
- expected marginal economics
- constraints
- evidence maturity

## Duration / CAP

Duration asks how long returns above the opportunity cost of capital can persist. Evidence should focus on observable moat outcomes rather than moat labels.

Useful outcomes:
- sustained pricing power without destructive churn
- retention / repeat behavior
- stable or rising share in a growing profit pool
- persistent superior gross/operating margins or asset turns
- cost/density advantages that strengthen with scale
- switching/embeddedness visible in customer behavior
- supply advantages or constrained assets

## Mean reversion challenge

High growth and high returns tend to attract competition and may mean-revert. A duration thesis must explain **why this company is an exception and what evidence would show the exception is fading**.

## Constraints

Always search for:
- saturation and cannibalization
- customer acquisition inflation
- supply/power/labor constraints
- management bandwidth
- regulation
- capital/funding constraints
- competitive response
- geographic localization risk


## Repeatability gate

Before converting a large opportunity set into long Duration, ask whether the engine can actually be repeated. For cohort/store/customer models, compare later vintages. For **product-cycle** models, compare successive generations after full R&D, launch, channel, inventory and cannibalization economics. If each cycle effectively resets product-market fit, keep Duration and Confidence constrained until cross-generation evidence improves.
