# Business Identity Scope

## Overview

Frame the company **before** market/TAM research. Determine what business it economically operates today, what customers hire it to do, which capabilities can transfer, and which future arenas are evidenced versus narrative.

Read `references/methodology-map.md`, `references/market-scope-classification.md`, and `references/evidence-standard.md`. Load other references only when their trigger applies.

## Required investigation

1. Separate **product definition**, **current economic business**, **customer job/occasion**, **capability definition**, and **management strategic direction**.
2. Use Who × What Need × How when boundaries are ambiguous. Treat sector/GICS as metadata, never as business identity.
3. Classify every arena independently as **PROVEN / EMERGING / OPTION / NARRATIVE**. Separately tag `arena_relationship` as CORE_EXTENSION, CORE_COMPLEMENT, ADJACENT_STANDALONE, ENABLING_INFRASTRUCTURE, or UNRESOLVED. Potential market size cannot upgrade evidence class.
4. Detect **Identity Transition** when capital, customers, revenue, organization, or economics are moving into a new arena.
5. Run the scope stress test: `too_narrow` → `candidate` → `too_broad`. Explain how each would change competitors/TAM/runway downstream.
6. Use structural analogues only after comparing customer job, economic unit, route-to-market, capital model, power source, growth mechanism, and geographic replication. Record transfer limits.
7. Preserve a main alternative frame, framing risks, critical unknowns, and **disconfirming evidence**.

## Output

Produce `business_identity_pack` exactly as defined in `../future-compounder/references/pipeline-contract.md`.

## DoD

The work is complete only when it can state:

> “For this analysis, we define the company as ______ because ______; ______ is EMERGING, ______ is OPTION, and ______ remains NARRATIVE/too broad and is excluded from core scope.”

The candidate definition must explain current economics without claiming profit pools the company has no evidence of capturing or double-counting a complement/enabling capability as an independent market.

**STOP:** Do not estimate TAM/category growth, forecast market share, judge compounder quality, perform valuation, or promote optionality into the base case.
