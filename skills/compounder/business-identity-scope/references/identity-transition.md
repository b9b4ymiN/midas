# Identity Transition Detection

A company can move from one economic identity to another. Detect transition rather than freezing the historical sector label.

## Evidence lanes
- **Capital shift:** material capex/R&D/M&A/headcount moving to the new arena.
- **Revenue shift:** new arena becomes economically material.
- **Customer shift:** new customer groups or occasions show repeat adoption.
- **Organization shift:** segment reporting, leadership, incentives, supply chain, or distribution change.
- **Economic shift:** new arena begins contributing profit, cash, or a defensible profit-pool position.

## State
- NONE
- EARLY
- DEVELOPING
- MATERIAL
- TRANSFORMATIVE
- UNRESOLVED

Management language alone cannot justify a state above EARLY.
