# Structural Analogue Protocol

Use analogues as hypothesis generators and outside-view references, not identity shortcuts.

Compare at least:
- customer job/occasion;
- economic unit;
- route-to-market;
- capital model;
- power/advantage source;
- growth mechanism;
- geographic replication logic.

Classify lesson transferability:
- HIGHLY_TRANSFERABLE
- PARTIAL
- WEAK
- MISLEADING

For each analogue record:
1. structural similarities;
2. material differences;
3. transferable lesson;
4. non-transferable lesson;
5. evidence that would make the analogy stronger or weaker.

Example: Starbucks may be PARTIAL for a premium-tea chain: useful for global beverage ritual/physical availability, but potentially weak for franchise economics or product-category behavior.
