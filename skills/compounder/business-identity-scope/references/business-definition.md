# Business Definition Protocol

## Five simultaneous views

Never start by selecting one sector label as truth. Build all five views first.

1. **Product definition** — what is sold today?
2. **Current economic business** — where do revenue, gross profit, cash, and invested capital actually come from?
3. **Customer job / occasion** — what progress or occasion causes the purchase, and what alternatives compete for that same job?
4. **Capability definition** — what repeatable organizational capabilities can travel beyond the current product?
5. **Strategic direction** — where is management actually committing capital, people, partnerships, and operating attention?

## Abell check: Who × What × How

For each candidate business definition record:
- WHO: customer groups that materially pay or influence economics.
- WHAT: customer function/job/occasion.
- HOW: technology, route-to-market, or solution architecture used to serve the need.

## Reconciliation

A candidate definition is useful when it:
- explains most current economics;
- includes demand-side substitutes relevant to customer choice;
- does not claim profit pools the company has no evidence of accessing;
- accommodates credible, evidenced identity transition without treating options as proven.

If views conflict, output **Identity Transition** or **Multiple Proven Arenas** rather than forcing one label.
