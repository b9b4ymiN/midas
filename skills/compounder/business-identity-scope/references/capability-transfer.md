# Capability Transfer Protocol

A capability is not a market right. Test claimed transferability through four questions.

1. **Demonstrated mastery** — has the company shown the capability repeatedly in the core business?
2. **Market access** — does the capability plausibly open multiple customer/market arenas?
3. **Customer benefit** — does it materially improve cost, performance, convenience, trust, speed, or another buyer outcome in the target arena?
4. **Defensibility** — is it difficult enough to replicate that the company could retain economics after entry?

Record each capability as:
- PROVEN_TRANSFERABLE
- PLAUSIBLE_TRANSFER
- WEAK_TRANSFER
- UNRESOLVED

A technically feasible adjacency remains OPTION/NARRATIVE until customers and economics validate the transfer.
