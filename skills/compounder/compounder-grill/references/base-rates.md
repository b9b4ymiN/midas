# Base Rates and Outside View

Base rates are a **prior**, not a hard reject. Their purpose is to prevent an attractive inside-view narrative from assuming that rare persistence is normal.

## Required outside-view questions

When material and evidence is available, ask:
- How persistent is high revenue/EPS growth in comparable firms/lifecycle stages?
- How persistent are high ROIC / margins / market share in this industry structure?
- What usually happens as firms move from early growth to scale/maturity?
- Which failure modes are common for this business model: CAC inflation, cohort decay, overcapacity, M&A dilution, regulation, leverage, commoditization?

Prefer empirical or broad-sample evidence over anecdotes. If no useful base-rate evidence exists, mark `UNRESOLVED` rather than inventing a pseudo-statistical prior.

## Bayesian update format

1. **Prior / outside view** — what tends to happen to comparable businesses?
2. **Company evidence** — what evidence suggests this case may be better/worse/different?
3. **Update** — how should duration/potential/confidence change?
4. **Residual uncertainty** — what still has not been tested across cohorts, geographies, competition, or cycles?

Base rates should challenge duration assumptions especially strongly when the thesis relies on long periods of exceptional growth or returns.
