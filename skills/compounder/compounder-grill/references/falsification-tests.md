# Falsification Tests

Run all applicable tests. “No evidence of failure” is not evidence of success.

## 1. Historical Growth Extrapolation
Is long-duration growth being inferred mainly from past CAGR? Use Growth Architecture, saturation, cohort evidence, and the outside-view **base rate**.

## 2. Growth Purchased with Capital
Is growth being purchased with disproportionate capex, working capital, discounting, CAC, M&A, leverage, or dilution without matching incremental NOPAT/FCF economics?

## 3. Marginal Return Decay
Are new cohorts/capacity/investments earning less or paying back more slowly than legacy assets despite flattering reported average ROIC?

## 4. Runway Illusion
Is headline TAM inaccessible because of share limits, regulation, local economics, capital needs, capacity, saturation, or cannibalization?

## 5. Moat Narrative Trap
Do claimed network effects, brand, switching costs, or scale advantages lack outcome evidence such as retention, pricing power, share persistence, or superior economics?

## 6. Scale Economics Failure
Do later units/cohorts deteriorate as expansion reaches weaker geographies/customers, acquisition cost rises, utilization falls, or central complexity overwhelms operating leverage?

## 7. Unit Economics Theater
Do attractive showcased units fail to translate to consolidated FCF/NOPAT and **per-share** owner economics after overhead, working capital, maintenance capex, and SBC/dilution?

## 8. Capital Allocation Failure
Is a good core engine offset by poor acquisition-cohort returns, overexpansion, leverage, dilution, or buybacks that fail to improve net diluted ownership?

## 9. Financial Resilience Failure
Is market runway larger than financeable runway because debt maturities, liquidity, working capital, refinancing, or external-equity dependence can interrupt high-return reinvestment?

## 10. Accounting / Intangible Distortion
Could expensed intangibles, leases, acquisitions, stock compensation, capitalization policies, or working capital make reported ROIC/margins misleading?

## 11. Mean Reversion / Base Rate
What attracts competitors or erodes returns? What outside-view evidence says exceptional economics usually decay, and why should this company be an exception?

## 12. Governance / Owner Trap
Is control aligned with minority owners? Review related parties, compensation, dilution, succession, communication honesty, and empire building.

## 13. Reverse Reality Failure
Does a 10x (or optional 100x) business-scale path require impossible share, margin, units/capacity, capital, funding, or dilution assumptions?

## 14. Unknown-Unknown Proxy
List thesis-critical assumptions without direct evidence. Identify the earliest observable signal that would validate or falsify each.

## Counter-thesis format

For every major hypothesis:
- hypothesis
- supporting evidence
- contradicting evidence
- unresolved evidence
- current verdict
- what would change the verdict

## 15. Product-Cycle Repeatability
For hardware, media hits, fashion drops, drug launches, game releases, or other generation-dependent growth, distinguish a repeatable Economic Unit from a product-cycle event. Ask whether economics have persisted across multiple generations after launch costs, channel inventory, cannibalization, refresh timing, competition, and per-share reinvestment. One successful cycle is operating evidence, not proof of long-duration repeatability.


## 16. Category Tailwind Illusion
Is company growth being inferred from a fast-growing category without proving profit capture, share mechanism, or company-specific economics? A category tailwind is an opportunity, not a return.

## 17. Share-Gain Quality Failure
Is share gain purchased through discounts, excessive capacity, subsidies, M&A, or channel spending that degrades unit/per-share economics? Reconcile Layer 1 share evidence with downstream returns.

## 18. Channel Incrementality Failure
Are delivery, app, marketplace, new-product, or new-format volumes mostly cannibalizing existing demand? Require evidence of new buyers/occasions, halo/recapture, and economic translation.

## 19. International Replication Illusion
Is foreign store/capacity growth being treated as proven replication without local customer adoption, productivity, localization, competitive, and economic evidence?

## 20. Metric Comparability Failure
Does a KPI trend depend on changed definitions, consolidation, revenue recognition, channel mix, or owned/franchise mix? If yes, downgrade the trend until normalized or label it not directly comparable.
