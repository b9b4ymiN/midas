# Reverse 10x / 100x Business Reality Check

This is **not valuation** and does not produce a target price. It stress-tests whether a large compounding narrative requires a physically/economically impossible future business.

## Principle

Start with a 10x scale/value-creation case because it is more decision-useful. Use 100x only as an optional extreme stress case when relevant to the user's question.

## Reverse-engineer the required business

Given the chosen horizon and target multiple, derive as transparently as evidence permits:
- required economic units / customers / capacity
- required revenue
- required normalized margin / NOPAT / FCF economics
- required market share / penetration
- capital required to build/acquire that scale
- financing/funding dependence
- diluted share count / dilution required
- reinvestment return and duration implied by the path

If current market capitalization is used as a scale anchor, label it as a current observed market fact; do not infer fair value or a target multiple from it.

## Reality tests

Flag the path when it requires, for example:
- >100% market share or implausible serviceable-market capture
- unit density/capacity beyond physical or regulatory constraints
- mature margins structurally above credible economics without a mechanism
- capital needs inconsistent with funding capacity
- dilution that destroys per-share compounding
- no plausible way to preserve incremental returns at the required scale

## Output states

- **PLAUSIBLE** — required business scale is demanding but economically coherent.
- **STRETCHED** — multiple assumptions require exceptional execution/persistence.
- **IMPLAUSIBLE** — one or more core requirements violate credible market/economic constraints.
- **UNRESOLVED** — insufficient evidence to perform a meaningful check.

Keep assumptions explicit and tagged. Do not use this check to claim a stock “will” 10x/100x.
