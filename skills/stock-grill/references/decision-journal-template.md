# Decision Journal Template (Layer 3)

The template for the output stock-grill writes at the end. Store it in **the stock's own analysis output** (e.g. next to the `both-stock-analysis` HTML report, or in a project's `theses/` folder) — **do not commit it back to the skills repo**.

Purpose: lock the belief ex-ante, before the outcome, to defeat "resulting" (judging the process by the outcome). Open it later to compare expectation vs reality.

---

## Template

```md
# Decision Journal — [COMPANY] ([TICKER]) — [YYYY-MM-DD]

## Context
[date · current price · short read on market/sector conditions right now]

## Variant perception
**The market thinks:** [X — priced in]
**I think:** [Y]
**Because:** [Z — why I am right and the market is wrong]

## Catalyst + deadline
[the catalyst that will close the gap · deadline — no deadline = wishful thinking]

## Time horizon
[the timeframe the thesis needs to play out]

## Valuation
- method: [DCF / relative / SOTP — which is primary]
- Bear / Base / Bull fair value: [X / Y / Z]
- current price: [P] · implied upside (base): [%]

## Sizing inputs
- max % of book: [%]
- hit rate / slugging / win-loss: [if you have a track record]

## Confidence
[% as a number — not high/med/low] — split by claim where possible:
- variant view: [%]
- primary valuation method: [%]
- catalyst will occur: [%]

## Pre-mortem — "down 40% in 12 months because..."
1. [failure cause — likelihood × impact]
2. [...]
3. [...]

## What would prove me wrong
[testable observations — if they occur, accept the thesis is wrong]
1. [...]
2. [...]

## Pre-committed sell triggers + review date
- (a) catalyst-fail: [concrete — what does not occur, by when]
- (b) thesis-broken: [which core assumption is no longer operative]
- (c) valuation-stretched: [what price is touched]
- stop (if any): [price] — does it match the kill-criteria? [Y/N + reason]
- review date: [YYYY-MM-DD]

## Not financial advice
Research and educational output only.
```

---

## How to fill it

- **Write it now, not later** — every field is filled before owning the position (or before deciding to keep holding). If fields cannot be filled, the thesis is not ready to decide on.
- **Real probabilities** — base is usually ≥ 50%; 33/33/33 means there is no real view.
- **Review later** — reopen this file when the outcome lands (earnings / deadline), compare expected vs actual, and separate luck from skill.
