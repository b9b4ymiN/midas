1 Business & Narrative

Thai Union operates four reporting segments: ambient seafood at 47.2 percent of gross revenue, frozen and chilled at 27.8 percent, pet food at 14.7 percent and value-added at 10.3 percent. The group buys tuna on the world market and sells into the United States and Europe, so its margin is set as much by raw-material cost and tariffs as by volume. Pet food is the segment growing fastest and carries a structurally higher margin than canned tuna.

2 Financial Dashboard

4 Earnings & Sentiment

The most recent quarter beat on revenue and missed on margin. Tuna prices climbed sharply in March and the company holds a two to four month cost buffer, so the raw-material effect lands next quarter rather than this one. Consensus has the stock priced for a modest recovery, not for perfection.
